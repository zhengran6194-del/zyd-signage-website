Product overview

A Consistent Sign Family for the Entire Building

Wayfinding is designed as a system rather than a single sign. Typography, colors, arrows, pictograms, mounting heights and information hierarchy are coordinated to guide visitors clearly through the space.

We can work from architectural plans, sign schedules or site photographs to develop a modular family of signs that is easy to update and maintain.

  • Room signs and floor directories
  • Braille and tactile options
  • Modular changeable information
  • Wall, suspended and freestanding types

Ask an Engineer
Can you develop a full sign schedule?

Yes. We can organize sign types, quantities, dimensions, copy and locations from your drawings or site information.

Do you support multilingual signs?

Yes. English, French, Arabic and other languages can be coordinated with appropriate font and layout checks.

Can information panels be replaced later?

Yes. Modular inserts or magnetic panels can be specified for names, floor plans and tenant information.

Do you provide installation location drawings?

We can provide elevation references, mounting details and sign-location schedules when the required architectural information is supplied.
