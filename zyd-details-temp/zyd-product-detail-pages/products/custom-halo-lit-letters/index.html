Product overview

Architectural Lettering Built for a Clean, Even Halo Glow

Halo-lit channel letters place the LED source behind the metal face, creating a refined glow around every character while keeping the front surface visually clean. The result is a premium architectural finish that remains legible without appearing overly bright.

Each letter is fabricated to the supplied logo or font. Return depth, stand-off distance, LED density, drainage and fixing points are engineered around the overall size, wall material and installation environment.

  • 304 or 316 stainless steel
  • 3000K / 4000K / 6500K LEDs
  • Stud, rail or back-panel mounting
  • IP65 outdoor electrical system

Ask an Engineer
Can halo-lit letters be used outdoors?

Yes. Outdoor sets use sealed LEDs, drainage details, weather-resistant wiring and coated metal. For coastal sites, 316 stainless steel and corrosion-resistant fixings are recommended.

How much wall clearance is required?

A typical halo stand-off is 15–30 mm. The exact distance depends on the desired glow, wall color and letter size.

Can you match our brand color?

Yes. We can match RAL, Pantone or physical color samples for painted surfaces and recommend the closest LED color.

Do you provide an installation template?

Yes. A full-size paper template, drilling points, wiring diagram and installation instructions can be included.
