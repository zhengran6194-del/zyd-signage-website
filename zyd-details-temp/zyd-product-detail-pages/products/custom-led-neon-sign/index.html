Product overview

The Neon Look with Modern LED Reliability

Flexible LED neon produces a continuous luminous line without exposed glass tubes. It is lightweight, low voltage and suitable for detailed logos, scripts and decorative graphics.

The neon path is bent and fixed to a laser-cut or CNC-routed acrylic backboard. Backboard shape, cable exit, dimmer, hanging points and wall fixings are customized to the installation.

  • 6–12 mm silicone neon flex
  • Transparent or colored acrylic backing
  • Dimmer and remote control options
  • Indoor and outdoor configurations

Frequently Asked Questions

Ask an Engineer
Can you convert any logo into neon?

Most logos can be adapted. Very small details may need simplification to maintain a continuous neon path and safe bending radius.

What file should I provide?

AI, EPS, PDF or SVG vector artwork is ideal. A clear PNG or JPG can also be used for an initial layout.

Is the sign supplied ready to install?

Yes. The neon sign can be pre-wired with the power supply, cable, switch or dimmer and mounting hardware.

Can a large sign be divided for shipping?

Yes. Large signs can be divided into aligned sections with concealed connectors and an installation template.
