Product overview

High-Impact Graphics in a Slim, Serviceable Structure

Our LED light boxes combine a fabricated aluminum or stainless-steel body with acrylic, fabric or tension-face graphics. Internal light distribution is engineered to minimize hotspots while maintaining a clean profile.

Each unit is made to order around the installation method, viewing distance, service access and local voltage. Single-sided, double-sided projecting and large-format configurations are available.

  • Single or double-sided designs
  • Acrylic, fabric or flex-face graphics
  • 12V / 24V LED illumination
  • Wall, hanging or freestanding mounting

Ask an Engineer
Can the graphic be replaced?

Yes. Magnetic, snap-frame, acrylic and fabric systems can all be designed for quick graphic replacement.

How thin can the light box be?

Small edge-lit units can be approximately 25–40 mm deep. Larger or brighter direct-lit units require more depth for uniform illumination.

Can you manufacture double-sided projecting signs?

Yes. We supply the sign body, wall bracket, LED system, power supply and mounting hardware as one coordinated set.

Is DDP shipping available?

DDP shipping can be quoted to many destinations, depending on product size, country and delivery address.
