Product overview

High-Visibility Outdoor Identity with Engineered Structure

Outdoor monument and pylon signs combine brand graphics, structural steel, weather-resistant cladding and optional illumination. Every project is customized to the site, viewing distance and installation method.

Ask an Engineer
Can you calculate wind load?

We can provide structural recommendations based on dimensions, installation location, wind speed and foundation information. Final local approval should be completed by a licensed engineer where required.

Which material is best near the coast?

316 stainless steel, marine-grade aluminum, suitable coatings and corrosion-resistant fasteners are normally recommended for salt-spray environments.

Can the sign be shipped in sections?

Yes. Large signs can be modularized with bolted internal joints, alignment features and labeled wiring.

Do you supply anchor bolts?

Anchor bolts, templates and base-plate drawings can be supplied according to the confirmed foundation design.
